# Creatine Brain Energy and Verbal Fluency Training

## Creatine for Brain Energy

Creatine is the most-discussed brain-energy intervention in current research:

- Crosses the blood-brain barrier
- Raises phosphocreatine levels in neurons
- Shown to slow early Alzheimer's cognitive decline by ~30%
- Benefits extend beyond muscle gains to cognitive function

## Verbal Fluency Training Activities

Solo exercises to improve word retrieval speed and connected speech:

### Describe-What-You-See (60-second rapid recall)
- Grab any object (mug, plant, photo) and describe it in full sentences for 60 seconds
- No pauses, no filler words
- Forces brain to pull words fast under time pressure
- Same mechanism as conversation but zero social pressure
- Do 3 times daily with different objects
