# Verbal Fluency Improvement Activities

Evidence-backed activities to improve verbal fluency and reduce word-finding difficulty, synthesized from 9 sources + 1 NHS clinical PDF + research citations.

## Top-Ranked Activities

**Rapid Category Naming (60s sprints):** Name items from a category (animals, foods) for 60 seconds. Start at 15-20/min, improve to 30+ with practice (FOKIQ; Wright et al. 1983).

**Word Games:** Crosswords, anagrams, Taboo, Articulate — exercise lexical access pathways. NHS Oxford Health recommends commercial word-finding games. Supported by Neuropsychology 2004.

**Retrieval Practice:** Prompt → pause 3-10s → speak out loud → check → correct. Trains the recall bottleneck that causes hesitation.

**Articulation Drills:** Tongue twisters, lip/tongue exercises, vocal warm-ups improve motor speech speed and clarity.

**Stream-of-Consciousness Speaking:** Timed, unstructured speaking (5 min) reduces inhibition and overthinking.

## Supporting Practices

Reading 30min/day builds vocabulary reservoir. Box breathing (4-4-4) before speaking regulates anxiety. Musical practice correlates with phonemic fluency in aging (Fauvel et al. 2015, arXiv).
